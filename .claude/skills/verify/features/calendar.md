# Calendar and day detail

The Calendar tab shows a month grid: each day's number sits in a progress ring (what was done over what the day asked), the ring turns violet once the hidden quest is done, and each epic is a hand-drawn marker stroke behind the dates from the day it was drawn to the day it was finished (or to its last day while unfinished), grey until done. A small purple dot under a date marks a day with a journal entry. Below the grid sit a legend and an `All journal entries` card, the only way into that list from this tab. Tapping a day opens a bottom sheet with that day's net coins, routines, quests, the night's journal entry, spending and ratings.

## Sub-features

- `cal-grid` month grid; today's number is in the accent colour (inside its progress ring); days after today are dimmed and not tappable, and an empty day does not open. A cell whose ring is empty can still open when the day has rows (driven: a past day with only an open routine and its penalty rows opened). The padding days of the neighbouring months are tappable too, at 0.4 opacity.
- `cal-nav` previous / next month chevrons (next is disabled on the current month; a `This month` pill returns from another month).
- `cal-marks` (since commit 178e324) a progress ring per day: done over asked, counting the random quests (a trivial group as one) and the clear-gating routines done on time (`CalendarMarks.progress`); the ring is violet once the hidden quest is done. An epic is a marker stroke from its draw day to its done day, or, while unfinished, to the Sunday of its last week (+7 days per extension); grey until done, then one of four colours in turn (`CalendarMarks.epicSpans`). The legend has four chips: `Quests and routines done`, `Hidden too`, `Epic, grey until done`, `Journal entry` (a purple dot). The old tier dots, journal pencil, routines tick and star are gone. A journal entry is the purple dot under the date, 4 / 5 / 6 pt for the three word-count bands (under 100, 100–299, 300+; none on a future day), and `journal entry` in the cell's accessibility value.
- `cal-detail` the day sheet (720 pt detent that can also be dragged to `.large`, drag indicator, **no `Done` button**: swipe down to close). Order: header (weekday, date, a `+N coins` pill that is the sum of that day's ledger rows, a tier pill reading `Running low` / `Low energy` / `Normal energy` / `High energy`, absent when the day has no day setting; the coin text reads `+1 coin`, `0 coins`), `Routines` (collapsible, open by default, `<done> / <total> done`), `Quests` (collapsible, **closed** by default, `<done> / <total> done`; the total counts every row that is not replaced, the epic and the hidden quest included, so it is the random slots plus 1 on the day the epic was drawn: read it, don't assert a fixed number), `THAT NIGHT` (night card, whose right side reads `<time> · <n> words`, with the excerpt and two pills, `Read` and `Edit`; or `Nothing written` + `Write about this day`; shown only for today and earlier days), `Done this day for another day`, `Spending, penalties, skips`, `Other ratings this day` (caption "Ratings given this day."), `Body` (only when a day setting exists). Row marks: check for done, ring for open, dash for replaced / swapped / rerolled, cross for cancelled / skipped / missed.
- `cal-entries` the `All journal entries` card (`N entries, newest first`, or `Nothing written yet`) opens [the entries page](./journal-entries.md). The header has no book button any more (removed in 856dec3); the page's title row is the word `Calendar` alone.
- `cal-missed` a routine nobody did reads `Did not finish` with a clay `-<penalty>` pill in the **Routines** section of its due day's sheet; the pill is the **running total** of its charges (11 + 16 + 21 = `-48` for a 21-point routine after all three rounds), while the coin pill of the due day holds only the round-1 charge (`-11 coins`) and rounds 2 and 3 sit under the later days' `Spending, penalties, skips`. Its `skip` ledger row (0 points) is dated due day + 3, not the next day, and appears on that later day's sheet. A routine that does not count toward clearing the day is never charged, so it shows no pill. The Calendar page itself has no `Did not finish` card any more (removed in commit c55f6ab).
- Ratings summary: the `Ratings` page no longer hangs off the Calendar; it is on [Trends](./trends.md).

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Tap the Calendar tab (x=160, y=880 pt); tap a day; tap the round `‹ ›` buttons to change month; tap the `All journal entries` card.

## Driving it with the simulator

Preconditions:

- `V up --fresh`, then complete one quest and rate it +2 ([today-complete](./today-complete.md)).

- **Grid (driven 2026-10-12).** Tap Calendar. The grid shows the current month; the app's day (accent number) has a partly filled ring after one or two completions, and the week's epic is a grey stroke from the app day to that Sunday. The legend reads `Quests and routines done`, `Hidden too`, `Epic, grey until done`, `Journal entry` (the fourth chip is new since that pass). A fresh store shows `All journal entries — Nothing written yet`. The Monday 12th sat at about 52, 353 pt (third row).
- **Detail.** Tap the app day's cell (locate it from the screenshot; Sunday the 4th sat at about 388, 255 pt). The sheet header shows the weekday in script over the date and the pills (`+125 coins` was 100 grant + 25 quest). The `Quests` header shows `1 / 3 done`; tap it to open the section, where the completed quest shows `+<points>` and `Rated +2`.
- **Cross-check.** `V sql "select ZDAYKEY, sum(ZPOINTS) from ZLEDGERENTRY group by ZDAYKEY"` for that day equals the coin pill (spending, penalties, skips and the grant included).
- **Night card.** With nothing written the card is an outlined `Nothing written` with `Write about this day →` (opens the editor for that day; `Done` there returns to the sheet). After writing, it becomes a dark card `THAT NIGHT <time> · <n> words` with a 3-line excerpt (text and answers, not the questions) and two pills, `Read` (opens the reader, see [journal-entries](./journal-entries.md)) and `Edit` (opens the editor for that day at once; `Done` there returns to the sheet). The excerpt itself is not tappable. After writing, the day's cell also carries the purple dot under its number (the dot is a few pixels: zoom a `V shot`, and tie it to the entry with `V sql "select ZDAYKEY from ZJOURNALENTRY"`).
- **Close.** Swipe the sheet down (from about y=300 to 900 pt); there is nothing to tap.
- **Missed routine (driven).** Needs a fixed-day routine open on a past day: on a Wednesday store `Take out the trash` is done, so first give another routine a due day (Library → `Change days`, see [library](./library.md); a Tue / Thu schedule makes it due on Thu D0+1). `V day 4` (the app day is D0+4) judges D0+3: open the Thursday cell (about 220, 319 pt in the second row; it carries no mark): header `-11 coins`, `Normal energy`, `Routines 0 / 2 done` with `Send job applications` `Did not finish` `-13` and `Take out the trash` `Did not finish` `-48` (cross icons), `THAT NIGHT` `Nothing written` + `Write about this day →`, and `Spending, penalties, skips` with `Penalty` `Take out the trash — overdue day 1` `-11`. `V ledger` shows the three `penalty` rows (10-08, 10-09, 10-10) and the `skip` row dated 10-11. For an occurrence due on D0 itself the same state is `V day 3`.

## Gotchas

- The coin pill for the first day includes the 100-coin starting grant, because the grant is dated that day (driven: `+119 coins` = 100 − 20 redeemed + 5 + 6 + 28; `V sql` agreed).
- The epic stroke starts on the day the epic was drawn and stops on the day it was finished; an unfinished one runs to its last day. `Seed 60 journal days` also writes done quests and routines, so it fills rings too.
- Rings show only for days with generated data; days after today are dimmed (not blank) and cannot be opened.
- A cell's accessibility value reads `N of M done`, then `hidden quest done`, `journal entry` and the epic when they apply; nothing here reads the accessibility tree, so prove a ring's fraction from the store (the day's done and asked rows), not from the drawing. `JournalMarks` (the three word-count bands) sets the dot's size (4 / 5 / 6 pt), so a short and a long entry differ only by a point or two: prove a day has a dot from the store, not from the pixel size.
- A recovery day ([recovery](./recovery.md)) has no mark of its own on the grid; its sheet's tier pill reads `Running low`, and no new epic stroke starts inside a stretch.
- Section state (`Routines` open, `Quests` closed) is held for the session; re-opening a sheet keeps what you toggled.
- Time-travelled days appear as ordinary days.
- A month with rich history for screenshots: Debug → `Seed 60 journal days` (simulator only; see [trends](./trends.md)) writes entries and day settings, or restore a snapshot through Settings → Restore from JSON…. Both leave fixture data behind, so `V up --fresh` afterwards.
- At accessibility sizes the month grid is capped at `xxxLarge` by design; the legend, header and entries card scale fully.
