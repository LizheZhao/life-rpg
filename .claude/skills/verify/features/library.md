# Library

Settings → Library lists quest and routine templates, grouped by difficulty (quests) or schedule (routines), with search, and has its own `+` to add a row without putting it on today's page. Opening a row shows its settings, schedule, rename / delete, ratings and notes. A deleted row moves to a collapsed `Archived` section at the bottom, where it can be restored. Ratings and notes are append-only and dated.

## Sub-features

- `lib-switch` the `Quests` / `Routines` pill picker (accessibility label `Library`) switches the list.
- `lib-tiers` quest tiers `Trivial`, `Easy`, `Medium`, `Hard`, `Epic` start collapsed with a count each; the open set survives a relaunch (`library.openQuestTiers`). The counts leave out deleted rows, so they equal Debug's "Quests by difficulty" only until the first delete (Debug counts archived rows too). A tier with no listed row has no header at all (delete every Epic and the `Epic` header goes), and an empty library or a search with no match shows `Nothing in the library yet` / `Nothing matches "<q>"`.
- `lib-search` pulling the list down reveals a search field; a match opens its section and the header taps do nothing while searching.
- `lib-detail` a row opens a detail page. Quest: summary card (title, tier pill), `Settings` card (`On`), rating row; a pencil at the top right (accessibility label `Rename or delete`) opens the manage sheet, `Add a note`, `Notes`, `Ratings`. A row the user added (`origin` user) also wears a `Mine` pill in the list and on the summary card, so its card is not title-only. Routine: summary card (title only, no tier pill), `Settings` card (`On`, then `Difficulty` Easy / Medium / Hard with a plain-words note), `Schedule` card (a pill, the week strip, `Comes up on …`, a `Change days` button), then the rating row, `Add a note`, `Notes`, `Ratings`. There is no `Pays` stepper any more and the Schedule card no longer lists a weekly target or whether it counts toward clearing the day.
- `lib-edit` the Settings card writes the library row. `Difficulty` sets the tier and the points to the middle of its range (E 10, M 21, H 38); the note under it reads `Pays N each time. Left undone, it costs a at the end of the day, b the next day and c the day after (they add up), then it is dropped. A change applies from the next time it comes up.` with N and the amounts taken from the row's real points (a flexible routine says what a session short of the week's target costs on Sunday; one that does not gate the day says it is never charged). `On` off sets `ZISACTIVE` to 0 and the row reads `inactive`; the switch is unreachable by tap (see Gotchas), so that effect is from source.
- `lib-days` `Change days` opens a `Schedule` sheet (Weekly / Monthly, the weekday chips, `Flexible`, N of M days), prefilled from the row; `Save` writes it and the detail page redraws at once. Reaches forward only. While a session of the routine is open the `Flexible` switch is disabled and a flexible week cannot ask for fewer days; the sheet says so. Not editable (no button): a lighter version and a spec the form cannot write back.
- `lib-add` the round `+` at the top right of the Library opens `New in library`: `What` (text), `Kind` (`Random quest` / `Routine`), `Tier` chips (`Trivial Easy Medium Hard Epic` for a quest, `Easy Medium Hard` for a routine), the quest's `Can be the hidden quest` / `Weekends only` (not shown for Epic) or the routine's schedule controls, one line saying what it will make, and `Add to library`. It never puts anything on today's page.
- `lib-rename` the detail's pencil (top right) opens a medium sheet titled `Edit` in the app's style: `Name` with the text field prefilled in a card, `History keeps the words it was made with.`, a clay `Delete` pill with `Deleting moves it to Archived, where it can be restored.`, `Save` at the bottom (dim until the name changes) and `Cancel` at the top left. The row keeps its id, the summary card's title and doodle follow at once (the navigation title stays the constant `Quest` / `Routine`), and the list shows the new words. A name another listed row already has is refused (`the library already has one with these words`) in a banner on the sheet; an empty name cannot be saved at all (`Save` stays dim), so `it needs a name` is Core-only. An archived row does not hold its words.
- `lib-delete` the sheet's `Delete` asks first on the same sheet (titled `Delete`): `Delete “<name>”?`, `It leaves the library and is never drawn or scheduled again. Your history, ratings and notes stay. You can bring it back from Archived at the bottom of the library.`, and two buttons side by side, `Cancel` (back to the name) and a clay `Delete`. Confirming archives the row (`ZISARCHIVED=1`, `ZISACTIVE=0`), pops back to the list and the tier count drops by one. A routine's lighter versions that no other live routine offers go with it.
- `lib-archived` `Archived` (a count, collapsed by default, remembered like the tiers; one per kind) lists the deleted rows dimmed with their tier and a `Restore` pill. `Restore` brings the row back active (`ZISARCHIVED=0`, `ZISACTIVE=1`, even if it was off before it was deleted). They are not links: there is no detail page for an archived row. Search filters the archived rows too, and a routine's archived lighter versions are listed here as rows of their own (tier pill only); restoring the routine restores them.
- `lib-starter` when the bundled starter library holds rows this library lacks, a card at the very top (above the pill picker; hidden while searching) reads `N new in the starter library` / `Look through them and add what you like`. It opens `New in the starter library`: `Cancel`, a caption (`Added to your library as they are; nothing you changed or deleted comes back.`), `Quests` (by tier) and `Routines` (lighter versions last, with a `lighter version` pill), every row ticked by default, `Hide these` and a bottom bar `Add N`. `Add` inserts only the ticked rows, keeps every existing row as it is, links a new lighter version to its routine and closes the sheet; the card disappears when nothing is left. A row that carries a starter key (renamed, off or deleted included) is never offered again; rows from before keys existed are keyed by their words the first time the Library opens (`ZSTARTERKEY`).
- `lib-seed-once` the CSVs only seed an empty store: relaunching after a rename or a delete leaves the row counts alone and never inserts the original wording again.
- `lib-rate` a rating tapped on the detail page appends a row; the old ones stay.
- `lib-note` `Add a note` appends a note; the old rows survive.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Tap the Settings tab → Library (first row); pull down for search; tap a row; the round `+` at the top right (about 398, 84 pt) adds a row; back with the round button top-left.

## Driving it with the simulator

Preconditions: `V up --fresh`, then complete and rate one quest so it has a rating.

**Coverage note.** The quest detail steps (`Detail`, `Rate again`, `Note`) were driven before the Settings card existed, so their coordinates are stale by one card (about 55–110 pt lower now): re-read them from a screenshot. The `+`, the epic and trivial adds, the routine detail, `Difficulty`, `Change days`, rename, delete and restore were driven on the current build.

- **List.** Tap the Settings tab (x=340, y=880 pt), then Library (about 220, 454 pt at the top of Settings). The quest list shows its five tier headers collapsed with counts `25 / 27 / 24 / 23 / 9` (the starter library; the author's earlier CSV seed gave `16 / 18 / 17 / 18 / 7`); the pill picker is below the title. Routines (picker, x=320) shows its groups (`Set days`, `Flexible`, `Monthly or longer`, `Lighter versions`, and `Every few days` / `Not scheduled` when non-empty) open, each row with a summary pill and a week strip; the detail page has a `Schedule` card.
- **Search.** Swipe down from about y=400 to y=700 pt, tap the field (about 220, 190 pt), type `sunset`. The `Easy` section opens and shows `Watch a sunset` with a `+2` badge (the latest rating). `X` top-right closes search.
- **Detail.** Tap the row (about 220, 267 pt with the keyboard up). The page shows the `Easy` pill, the rating row with the right-most thumb filled and `Latest: Loved it, <day>. Rating again adds a new entry; the old ones stay.`, `Add a note`, and `Ratings` with `Loved it`, `+2`, `<day> · right after doing it`.
- **Rate again.** Tap the middle `–` button (about 220, 277 pt): a new row appears, `Latest: Fine`. `V sql "select ZRATING, ZTARGETKINDRAW, ZQUESTID is not null from ZQUESTRATING order by Z_PK"` shows the original `2 | quest | 1` and a new `0 | quest | 0`: a rating given here has no `ZQUESTID`.
- **Routines (driven).** Tap the `Routines` segment (about 320, 180 pt). The list shows `Set days` (3), `Flexible` (7) and below them `Monthly or longer` (2) and `Lighter versions` (7) on the starter library, all open; each row has a doodle, a summary pill (`Mon · Thu`, `Wed · Sat`, `Sat`, `Sun`) and an `M T W T F S S` strip with the due days filled. The starter library has no inactive routine; turn one off in its detail (the `On` switch is unreachable by tap) or take one from an old store to see the `inactive` pill and the dimmed row.
- **Routine detail (driven).** Tap `Take out the trash` (about 220, 445 pt). Page title `Routine`; the summary card is the title alone. `Settings`: `On`, a divider, `Difficulty` with the Easy / Medium / Hard pill control (about y=366 pt) and the note under it. `Schedule`: the pill `Wed · Sat`, the strip, `Comes up on Wednesday and Saturday.` and `Change days` (about 90, 664 pt). Then `How do you feel about it now?`. A seed routine that pays 5 shows Easy selected and the note says `Pays 5 …` (its real points) until the tier is changed.
- **Difficulty (driven).** Tap `Medium` (about 220, 366 pt). `V sql "select ZTEXT, ZDIFFICULTYRAW, ZBASEPOINTS from ZROUTINETASK where ZTEXT='Take out the trash'"` returns `medium | 21`, and the note redraws as `Pays 21 each time. Left undone, it costs 11 at the end of the day, 16 the next day and 21 the day after (they add up), …`. Easy gives 10 (5, 8, 10) and Hard 38 (19, 29, 38).
- **Change days (driven).** Tap `Change days`: the sheet opens with `Weekly` and Wed and Sat selected. Tap Wed (about 182, 290 pt) and Sat (368) off and Tue (119) and Thu (244) on; the caption reads `Due Tue, Thu. From tomorrow on: …`. Tap `Save` (about 220, 888 pt). `V sql "select ZSPEC, ZWEEKLYTARGET, ZFLEXIBLEWITHINWEEK, ZDIFFICULTYRAW, ZBASEPOINTS from ZROUTINETASK where ZTEXT='Take out the trash'"` returns `TUE,THU | 2 | 0 | medium | 21` (the target follows the days, the tier is untouched) and the detail page shows `Tue · Thu` and `Comes up on Tuesday and Thursday.`
- **Add an epic (driven).** Tap `+`, tap the text field (about 260, 205 pt) and type `Write a short story`, wait a second (the doodle changes to a notebook), tap `Epic` (about 306, 408 pt): the hidden / weekend switches disappear and the line reads `Joins the pool the weekly epic is drawn from; pays 60–150 when done.` Tap `Add to library` (about 220, 575 pt with the keyboard up). The `Epic` header goes from 7 to 8, `V sql "select ZTEXT, ZDIFFICULTYRAW, ZHIDDENELIGIBLE, ZWEEKENDONLY, ZORIGINRAW from ZQUESTTEMPLATE where ZORIGINRAW='user'"` returns `epic | 0 | 0 | user`, and no `DailyQuest` row exists for it (nothing was added to today).
- **On switch (not reachable by tap).** The `On` toggle (about 382, 278 pt) did not flip under the control tool, so `ZISACTIVE=0` and the `inactive` pill after switching a row off were not reproduced; they are from source. The same goes for `Flexible` and the hidden / weekend switches in the forms.
- **Rename (driven).** Open a seed quest (Epic header about 76, 480 pt, then the first row, `A full day with only essential screen use`, about 191, 550 pt). Tap the pencil (about 398, 84 pt): the `Edit` sheet opens with `Cancel`, `Name`, the prefilled field, `History keeps the words it was made with.`, `Delete` and a dim `Save`. Tap the name (about 330, 603 pt), type ` (renamed)`, wait a second (the sheet moves up with the keyboard), tap `Save` (about 220, 577 pt). `V sql "select hex(ZID), ZTEXT, ZISARCHIVED from ZQUESTTEMPLATE where ZTEXT like '%full day%'"` returns one row with the id it had before and `A full day with only essential screen use (renamed)`; the summary card shows the new title at once.
- **Delete, Archived, Restore (driven).** Pencil → `Delete` (about 72, 706 pt with the keyboard down): the sheet turns into `Delete “<name>”?` with `Cancel` (about 119, 879 pt) and a clay `Delete` (about 320, 879 pt). `Cancel` returns to `Edit` and `ZISARCHIVED` stays 0. `Delete` again, then the clay `Delete`: the page pops back, `Epic` drops by one (9 → 8 on the starter library), `V sql` still shows every row with `ZISARCHIVED=1`, `ZISACTIVE=0` for this one, and an `Archived` header with `1` sits under the tiers (about 76, 538 pt with Epic closed, 480 pt for Epic itself). Open it: a dimmed row with `Restore` (about 370, 618 pt). `V relaunch` first and the row count is unchanged (nothing is reseeded). `Restore` sets `ZISARCHIVED=0`, `ZISACTIVE=1` and the row is back under Epic.
- **Add a trivial quest (driven).** `+`, type `Stretch for one minute` (about 260, 216 pt), tap `Trivial` (about 44, 431 pt, visible above the keyboard), `Add to library` (about 220, 577 pt). `V sql` returns `trivial | 0 | user` for it and `ZDAILYQUEST` still has today's 3 rows.
- **Starter card (driven).** Restore a backup that predates `starterKey` (or one taken from a store whose library has diverged from `doc/starter_library.json`), then open the Library: the card is at about y=198 pt (tap 220, 198; the live screen, `V shot`, shows it, the control tool's own screenshot lags). `V sql "select count(*), sum(ZSTARTERKEY is not null) from ZQUESTTEMPLATE"` after opening: every row that matches a starter row by its words now has a key. The author's phone library offered 9 routines (the starter's general ones, plus `Clean and tidy the apartment (vacuum, dishes, dust)`, which was reworded there). `Add 9` (about 220, 888 pt) took the routines from 21 to 30, the new parents carry a lighter-version link (`length(ZDOWNGRADEIDS) > 0`), the card is gone and `Flexible` / `Set days` counts grew. A fresh install is offered nothing. **V9 → V10 upgrade (driven):** there is no V9 build to install, so a scratch copy of the repo with `starterKey` turned into a computed (not stored) property in both models produces a V9-shaped store; install that build, make some history, record a fingerprint of `ZQUESTTEMPLATE`, `ZROUTINETASK` (with `hex(ZDOWNGRADEIDS)`), `ZDAILYQUEST`, `ZLEDGERENTRY`, `ZJOURNALENTRY`, then `xcrun simctl install` the real build over it without uninstalling: `ZSTARTERKEY` appears on both library tables (null), the fingerprint is unchanged, and the first time the Library opens every matching row is keyed.
- **Note.** Tap the field (`What worked, what didn't`, about 220, 430 pt), type a note and wait for the text to land before tapping `Add note` (about 364, 493 pt; it is disabled while the field is empty). The field clears and keeps focus, and a `Notes` section appears. `V sql "select ZCOMMENT, ZTEXTSNAPSHOT from ZQUESTCOMMENT order by Z_PK"` returns it, and a second note adds a second row. On the list the row now shows a `2 notes` pill and the badge `0`.

## Gotchas

- Notes and ratings are never overwritten: assert row counts grow, not that a row changed.
- The control tool's `text` returns before every character is typed: a tap on `Add note` straight after it saved `second` and left `note` in the field. Screenshot (or wait a second) between typing and tapping.
- Search filters quest and routine text, not notes; while searching, header taps do nothing and the remembered open set is left alone.
- A quest rating also runs `Affinity.sync` (`ZAFFINITY` on the template follows the mean of its last three ratings); a routine rating does not.
- `Toggle` switches do not respond to the control tool's tap (see [rewards](./rewards.md)); steppers, buttons, pills and segmented controls do.
- Not driven: `Hide these` (it stores the ids in `UserDefaults` key `starter.dismissed`, which the backup does not carry), unticking single rows, renaming or deleting a routine (a quest was driven; the lighter-version cascade is covered by Core tests), the taken-name refusal, a routine add from the `+`, and the open-session lock in the schedule sheet (covered by Core tests). Switching Kind to Routine in the add sheet resets a Trivial / Hard / Epic tier to Easy.
- Return in the note field inserts a newline (it is multi-line with no submit action); only `Add note` saves.
- The Settings tab keeps a pushed page (Library, Debug …) when you switch tabs and come back; check the page before tapping a row.
- Affinity (the mean of the last three ratings) feeds the draw; prove it with `swift test`, not here.
