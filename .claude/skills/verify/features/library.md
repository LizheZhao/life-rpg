# Library

Settings → Library lists quest and routine templates, grouped by difficulty (quests) or schedule (routines) or, by choice, in one flat list from the best rated down, with search, and has its own `+` to add a row without putting it on today's page. Opening a row shows its settings, schedule, rename / delete, ratings and notes. A deleted row moves to a collapsed `Archived` section at the bottom, where it can be restored, and an `Undo` bar offers it back for a few seconds first. Ratings and notes are append-only and dated.

## Sub-features

- `lib-switch` the `Quests` / `Routines` pill picker (accessibility label `Library`) switches the list. A second pill picker under it, `Grouped` / `Rated best` (accessibility label `Order`), picks the layout; it starts on `Grouped` and is not remembered.
- `lib-rated` `Rated best` replaces the tier / schedule sections with one flat list of the shown kind: rows with a rating first, the best mean first, then the one rated more often, then A to Z, and unrated rows after them A to Z. A rated row carries a pill `<mean> · <n>×` instead of the latest-rating badge (the mean over every rating the row has, one decimal and a sign, e.g. `+1.0 · 1×`; green above 0, clay below, plain at 0). Rows open the detail page as in `Grouped`, search applies, and `Archived` stays below the list. A rating with no library row behind it (a custom task) counts for nothing. Trends' `Ratings` row opens the Library already on `Rated best` (not driven; it replaced the old `Ratings` summary page).
- `lib-tiers` quest tiers `Trivial`, `Easy`, `Medium`, `Hard`, `Epic` start collapsed with a count each; the open set survives a relaunch (`library.openQuestTiers`). The counts leave out deleted rows, so they equal Debug's "Quests by difficulty" only until the first delete (Debug counts archived rows too). A tier with no listed row has no header at all (delete every Epic and the `Epic` header goes), and an empty library or a search with no match shows `Nothing in the library yet` / `Nothing matches "<q>"`.
- `lib-search` pulling the list down reveals a search field; a match opens its section and the header taps do nothing while searching.
- `lib-detail` a row opens a detail page. Quest: summary card (title, tier pill), `Settings` card (`On`, a divider, `Tier` with five chips and a one-line note), rating row; a pencil at the top right (accessibility label `Rename or delete`) opens the manage sheet, `Add a note`, `Notes`, `Ratings`. A row the user added (`origin` user) also wears a `Mine` pill in the list and on the summary card, so its card is not title-only. Routine: summary card (title only, no tier pill), `Settings` card (`On`, then `Difficulty` Easy / Medium / Hard with a plain-words note), `Schedule` card (a pill, the week strip, `Comes up on …`, a `Change days` button), then the rating row, `Add a note`, `Notes`, `Ratings`. There is no `Pays` stepper any more and the Schedule card no longer lists a weekly target or whether it counts toward clearing the day.
- `lib-edit` the Settings card writes the library row. A routine's `Difficulty` sets the tier and the points to the middle of its range (E 10, M 21, H 38); the note under it reads `Pays N. Skip it and it costs a, then b, then c on the next two days, then it's gone.` with N and the amounts taken from the row's real points (a flexible routine reads `Pays N. Each session short of the week's target costs X on Sunday.`; one that does not gate the day reads `Pays N. No penalty. Gone after three days.`, or `… Gone on Sunday.` when flexible). A quest's `Tier` chips (`Trivial Easy Medium Hard Epic`, the current one selected) set `ZDIFFICULTYRAW` at once (`LibraryEdit.setDifficulty`); the pill at the top of the page follows live, the line under the chips reads `Pays by tier.` (`Pays 4 each, drawn in threes.` for Trivial, `Pays 60–150.` for Epic), and moving to Epic clears `ZHIDDENELIGIBLE` and `ZWEEKENDONLY`. Both reach forward only: a quest already drawn keeps its slot and points. `On` off sets `ZISACTIVE` to 0 and the row reads `off` (it read `inactive` before 4987f0f; the "Off: not drawn…" and "Inactive — not drawn any more" lines are gone); the switch is unreachable by tap (see Gotchas), so that effect is from source.
- `lib-days` `Change days` opens a `Schedule` sheet (Weekly / Monthly, the weekday chips, `Flexible`, N of M days), prefilled from the row; `Save` writes it and the detail page redraws at once. The caption under the strip is the plain `Due Tue, Thu.` now. Reaches forward only. While a session of the routine is open the `Flexible` switch is disabled and a flexible week cannot ask for fewer days; the sheet says `Finish or skip the open one first.` Not editable (no button): a lighter version and a spec the form cannot write back.
- `lib-add` the round `+` at the top right of the Library opens `New in library`: `What` (text), `Kind` (`Random quest` / `Routine`), `Tier` chips (`Trivial Easy Medium Hard Epic` for a quest, `Easy Medium Hard` for a routine), the quest's `Can be the hidden quest` / `Weekends only` (not shown for Epic) or the routine's schedule controls, one line saying what it pays (`Pays by tier.`, `Pays 4 each, drawn in threes.` for Trivial, `Pays 60–150.` for Epic, `<when> · pays N.` for a routine), and `Add to library`. It never puts anything on today's page.
- `lib-rename` the detail's pencil (top right) opens a medium sheet titled `Edit` in the app's style: `Name` with the text field prefilled in a card, a clay `Delete` pill (no caption under it or under the name since 4987f0f), `Save` at the bottom (dim until the name changes) and `Cancel` at the top left. The row keeps its id, the summary card's title and doodle follow at once (the navigation title stays the constant `Quest` / `Routine`), and the list shows the new words. A name another listed row already has is refused (`the library already has one with these words`) in a banner on the sheet; an empty name cannot be saved at all (`Save` stays dim), so `it needs a name` is Core-only. An archived row does not hold its words.
- `lib-delete` the sheet's `Delete` archives the row at once, with no confirmation page (`ZISARCHIVED=1`, `ZISACTIVE=0`; history, ratings and notes stay), closes the sheet and pops the detail page back to the list, where the tier count drops by one. An `UndoBar` appears above the floating tab bar for about 6 s: an ink capsule reading `Deleted “<name>”` with an underlined `Undo`. `Undo` brings the row back (`ZISARCHIVED=0`, `ZISACTIVE=1`, and a row that was off before it was deleted is turned off again); after the 6 s only `Archived` → `Restore` does. A routine's lighter versions that no other live routine offers go with it. Owner-driven 2026-10-12.
- `lib-archived` `Archived` (a count, collapsed by default, remembered like the tiers; one per kind) lists the deleted rows dimmed with their tier and a `Restore` pill. `Restore` brings the row back active (`ZISARCHIVED=0`, `ZISACTIVE=1`, even if it was off before it was deleted). They are not links: there is no detail page for an archived row. Search filters the archived rows too, and a routine's archived lighter versions are listed here as rows of their own (tier pill only); restoring the routine restores them.
- `lib-starter` when the bundled starter library holds rows this library lacks, a card at the very top (above the pill picker; hidden while searching) reads `N new in the starter library` / `Look through them and add what you like`. It opens `New in the starter library`: `Cancel`, a caption (`Pick what you want.`), `Quests` (by tier) and `Routines` (lighter versions last, with a `lighter version` pill), every row ticked by default, `Hide these` and a bottom bar `Add N`. `Add` inserts only the ticked rows, keeps every existing row as it is, links a new lighter version to its routine and closes the sheet; the card disappears when nothing is left. A row that carries a starter key (renamed, off or deleted included) is never offered again; rows from before keys existed are keyed by their words the first time the Library opens (`ZSTARTERKEY`).
- `lib-seed-once` the starter library (`doc/starter_library.json`, `StarterLibrary.install`) only seeds an empty store: relaunching after a rename or a delete leaves the row counts alone and never inserts the original wording again.
- `lib-rate` a rating tapped on the detail page appends a row; the old ones stay.
- `lib-note` `Add a note` appends a note; the old rows survive.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Tap the Settings tab → Library (first row); pull down for search; tap a row; the round `+` at the top right (about 398, 84 pt) adds a row; back with the round button top-left.

## Driving it with the simulator

Preconditions: `V up --fresh`, then complete and rate one quest so it has a rating.

**Coverage note.** Coordinates here come from earlier builds. The quest detail steps (`Detail`, `Rate again`, `Note`) were driven before the Settings card existed, so they were stale by one card, and the card has since gained the `Tier` row. The list gained the `Grouped` / `Rated best` picker under `Quests` / `Routines`, so every list coordinate (search results, tier headers, rows, `Archived`, `Restore`) now sits one picker lower, and the `Edit` sheet lost its two captions and its confirmation page. Re-read positions from a screenshot. The `+`, the epic and trivial adds, the routine detail, `Difficulty`, `Change days`, rename and restore were driven before 4987f0f; the owner drove `Rated best`, the `Tier` chips and `Delete` with `Undo` on 2026-10-12. Not driven: Trends → `Ratings` into the Library.

- **List.** Tap the Settings tab (x=340, y=880 pt), then Library (the first row under the Recovery section; it sat at about 220, 650 pt before the Recovery footer was shortened, so read it from a screenshot). The quest list shows its five tier headers collapsed with counts `25 / 27 / 24 / 23 / 9` (the starter library; the author's earlier CSV seed gave `16 / 18 / 17 / 18 / 7`); the `Quests` / `Routines` picker is below the title, with the `Grouped` / `Rated best` picker under it. Routines (picker, x=320) shows its groups (`Set days`, `Flexible`, `Monthly or longer`, `Lighter versions`, and `Every few days` / `Not scheduled` when non-empty) open, each row with a summary pill and a week strip; the detail page has a `Schedule` card.
- **Search.** Swipe down from about y=400 to y=700 pt, tap the field (about 220, 190 pt), type `sunset`. The `Easy` section opens and shows `Watch a sunset` with a `+2` badge (the latest rating). `X` top-right closes search.
- **Detail.** Tap the row (about 220, 267 pt with the keyboard up, before the `Order` picker was added). The page shows the `Easy` pill, the rating row with the right-most thumb filled and `Latest: Loved it, <day>.`, `Add a note`, and `Ratings` with `Loved it`, `+2`, `<day> · right after doing it`.
- **Rate again.** Tap the middle `–` button (about 220, 277 pt): a new row appears, `Latest: Fine`. `V sql "select ZRATING, ZTARGETKINDRAW, ZQUESTID is not null from ZQUESTRATING order by Z_PK"` shows the original `2 | quest | 1` and a new `0 | quest | 0`: a rating given here has no `ZQUESTID`.
- **Rated best (owner-driven 2026-10-12).** With a rated row from the steps above, tap `Rated best` under the `Quests` / `Routines` picker: the tier sections give way to one flat list, the rated row first with a pill like `+1.0 · 1×` (the mean of its ratings, then how many), the unrated rows after it A to Z; a tap on a row opens its detail. Cross-check the pill: `V sql "select hex(ZTARGETID), avg(ZRATING), count(*) from ZQUESTRATING where ZTARGETID is not null group by 1"`. `Grouped` returns to the tiers (the choice is not remembered across a relaunch).
- **Quest tier (owner-driven 2026-10-12).** On a quest's detail the `Settings` card shows `On`, a divider, then `Tier` with the chips `Trivial Easy Medium Hard Epic`. Tap another chip: the pill at the top changes at once, the line under the chips reads `Pays by tier.`, and `V sql "select ZTEXT, ZDIFFICULTYRAW from ZQUESTTEMPLATE where ZTEXT='<title>'"` shows the new tier. Back on the list the row sits under its new tier header and the two counts moved by one; quests already drawn for today are untouched.
- **Routines (driven).** Tap the `Routines` segment (about 320, 180 pt). The list shows `Set days` (3), `Flexible` (7) and below them `Monthly or longer` (2) and `Lighter versions` (7) on the starter library, all open; each row has a doodle, a summary pill (`Mon · Thu`, `Wed · Sat`, `Sat`, `Sun`) and an `M T W T F S S` strip with the due days filled. The starter library has no inactive routine; turn one off in its detail (the `On` switch is unreachable by tap) or take one from an old store to see the `off` pill and the dimmed row.
- **Routine detail (driven).** Tap `Take out the trash` (about 220, 445 pt). Page title `Routine`; the summary card is the title alone. `Settings`: `On`, a divider, `Difficulty` with the Easy / Medium / Hard pill control (about y=366 pt) and the note under it. `Schedule`: the pill `Wed · Sat`, the strip, `Comes up on Wednesday and Saturday.` and `Change days` (about 90, 664 pt). Then `How do you feel about it now?`. A seed routine that pays 5 shows Easy selected and the note says `Pays 5 …` (its real points) until the tier is changed.

- **Starter card (driven).** Restore a backup that predates `starterKey` (or one taken from a store whose library has diverged from `doc/starter_library.json`), then open the Library: the card is at about y=198 pt (tap 220, 198; the live screen, `V shot`, shows it, the control tool's own screenshot lags). `V sql "select count(*), sum(ZSTARTERKEY is not null) from ZQUESTTEMPLATE"` after opening: every row that matches a starter row by its words now has a key. The author's phone library offered 9 routines (the starter's general ones, plus `Clean and tidy the apartment (vacuum, dishes, dust)`, which was reworded there). `Add 9` (about 220, 888 pt) took the routines from 21 to 30, the new parents carry a lighter-version link (`length(ZDOWNGRADEIDS) > 0`), the card is gone and `Flexible` / `Set days` counts grew. A fresh install is offered nothing. **V9 → V10 upgrade (driven):** there is no V9 build to install, so a scratch copy of the repo with `starterKey` turned into a computed (not stored) property in both models produces a V9-shaped store; install that build, make some history, record a fingerprint of `ZQUESTTEMPLATE`, `ZROUTINETASK` (with `hex(ZDOWNGRADEIDS)`), `ZDAILYQUEST`, `ZLEDGERENTRY`, `ZJOURNALENTRY`, then `xcrun simctl install` the real build over it without uninstalling: `ZSTARTERKEY` appears on both library tables (null), the fingerprint is unchanged, and the first time the Library opens every matching row is keyed.

## Gotchas

- Notes and ratings are never overwritten: assert row counts grow, not that a row changed.
- The control tool's `text` returns before every character is typed: a tap on `Add note` straight after it saved `second` and left `note` in the field. Screenshot (or wait a second) between typing and tapping.
- Search filters quest and routine text, not notes; while searching, header taps do nothing and the remembered open set is left alone.
- A quest rating also runs `Affinity.sync` (`ZAFFINITY` on the template follows the mean of its last three ratings); a routine rating does not.
- `Toggle` switches do not respond to the control tool's tap (see [rewards](./rewards.md)); steppers, buttons, pills and segmented controls do.
- Not driven: `Hide these` (it stores the ids in `UserDefaults` key `starter.dismissed`, which the backup does not carry), unticking single rows, renaming or deleting a routine (a quest was driven; the lighter-version cascade is covered by Core tests), the taken-name refusal, a routine add from the `+`, and the open-session lock in the schedule sheet (covered by Core tests). Switching Kind to Routine in the add sheet resets a Trivial or Epic tier to Easy; Hard is a routine tier and stays.
- Return in the note field inserts a newline (it is multi-line with no submit action); only `Add note` saves.
- The Settings tab keeps a pushed page (Library, Debug …) when you switch tabs and come back; check the page before tapping a row.
- Affinity (the mean of the last three ratings) feeds the draw; prove it with `swift test`, not here.
