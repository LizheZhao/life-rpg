# Today: complete a quest

The Today tab is a scroll of cards: header (avatar, a round book button, `+`), greeting, a dark journal card (`tonight` from 20:00, `today` when shown by day because the day is cleared), the level card, an `Epic` section with a routine-style card (tap the body to expand), Routines (full-width rows, overdue ones pinned first), Today's quests (full-width tinted rows), a `Hidden` section, then Completed and Ahead this week (each a stack). There is no Backlog section: missed routines show on their due day's sheet on the Calendar. The round complete button on a quest or routine row asks for confirmation, writes a ledger entry, plays the payout reveal (quests only; a routine's fixed payout goes straight to `+N` and `paid`) and offers a rating, all in one centered card. The coin count on the level card must equal the ledger.

## Sub-features

- `today-render` shows the level card, the epic card, `Routines`, `Today's quests` for the app day.
- `today-complete` completes a random slot: the centered card (`?` + `Complete` + `Not yet`), then in place rolling, landed (`+N`), rate; ledger row, level card update, hand line `1 day streak`.
- `today-rate` logs a rating (−2…+2) from the card's rate phase (pick, then `Done`; a tap on the dim or 8 s later leaves with the pick, or without one).
- `today-routine-complete` completes a routine row (open, and its late/lighter variants).
- `today-menu` the `⋯` on a row or the epic card opens a popover (a long-press on the card opens the system context menu instead, with rows titled `Reroll · 10` / `Reroll · free` and no price pill; a blocked row is just disabled). Quest: `Reroll`, `Cancel`, `Replace`, `Rate / note`, `Turn off`, then `Open link` when the quest has one. Routine (in the order the source builds it): `Cancel`, `Replace`, `Remove` (extras only), `Rate / note`, `Turn off`. Epic: `Reroll`, `Extend`, `Replace`, `Rate / note`, `Turn off`, `Open link`. Only `Reroll`, `Cancel` and `Extend` carry a price pill (`10 coins`, `free`); a blocked one is greyed and keeps it. Items are conditional: `Cancel` only on Easy / Medium / Hard quests and fixed routines (200 coins for a routine), `Replace` only where the add rules allow, and `Reroll` / `Extend` disappear once done; `Turn off` becomes `Turn on` for a row that is off (pill `turned off`). A hidden quest's menu is `Rate / note` and `Turn off`, a micro-action group's is `Reroll` and `Replace`, an Ahead row's is `Replace`, `Rate / note` and `Turn off`. Rows in the Completed stack carry only `Rate / note`, except a done epic with a link, which keeps `Open link`.
- `today-ahead` the `Ahead this week` header (`6 open`, clay `−13 Sun night`) over a stack of rows; tap the stack or the header to fan out, `Collapse` to fold; `Do now` on a candidate raises the same centered card (header, `+N` pill, `Complete`, `Not yet`) and a `routine` ledger row follows. Weekdays start collapsed (driven on a Wednesday: `5 open`, tap the header at about 90, 667 pt and the rows fan out, the chevron turns), Saturday and Sunday open. Candidates are the active flexible routines still short of their target with a later due day this week, so the section exists Monday to Saturday and is empty on a Sunday; the clay `−N Sun night` figure counts only open rows from earlier days.
- `today-add` the `+` sheet (`Add for today`) adds extra work on top of the day: `From library` (a routine) or `Custom`. Driven for the library path (the sheet and its caption on the current build; the add itself in an earlier pass). After a row is picked the bar above `Add` reads `<routine> · final, it stays on today's page`.
- `today-journal` the book button and the dark journal card open the journal editor (see [journal-editor](./journal-editor.md)).
- `today-levels` the level card (or the avatar, about 44, 92 pt) opens the Levels sheet (driven): `Levels`, a standing card (`Lv 1`, `0 earned`, `60 to Lv 2`, 20 dots), `Perks` with seven locked rows (`The day's first reroll is free` Lv 3, `The epic can be extended three times` Lv 5, `A late make-up pays 60% instead of 50%` Lv 8, `One free streak freeze a month` Lv 10, `Epic reroll 80 → 40` Lv 12, `The day's first two rerolls are free` Lv 15, `Streak freeze 300 → 150` Lv 20, each with `Lv N · X earned`) and a full-width `Done` pill at the bottom (about 220, 890 pt).
- `today-completed` a done card stays in its own section for about 0.9 s (check, strikethrough and `+N` finish there) and then moves to the `Completed` stack after Today's quests / Hidden: header `<n> done · +<sum>` equals today's done ledger rows, with two exceptions the source makes (the live epic is counted even when it was done on an earlier day this week, and a routine done ahead stays in Ahead), tap the stack or the header to fan out, `Collapse` or the header to fold, collapsed again after `V relaunch`. The section it left reads `<n> / <m> done`, or `cleared` when nothing is open.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open the app; the Today tab is first (x=100, y=880 pt).
- Tap the round button on a quest or routine row.
- Tap `⋯` on a row or the epic card (or long-press it); tap `+` (Add for today) top-right (about 396, 92 pt).
- Tap the level card or the avatar (about 44, 92 pt) to open the level track sheet.
- Tap the book button (about 342, 92 pt, accessibility label `Journal`) or the dark journal card for the journal.

## Driving it with the simulator

Preconditions:

- `V up --fresh`; `V doctor` shows `offset: 0` and note `app day`.
- The level card reads `100 coins`, `60 to Lv 2`, `normal day`, and `<n> random slots` (`1 random slot` when it is one); the hand line has no streak part. The slot count follows the day's routine load: 3 on a quiet weekday, 1 on a Saturday (6 routines), 2 on a Sunday (4); Wednesday 2026-10-07 had 3 routines and 2 random slots, plus the epic.
- From 20:00 until 05:00 (or once the day is cleared) a dark journal card (script label `tonight`, `Write about today`, `Start writing`, or `Keep going · N words` / `Written · N words` once there is text; a purple dot on the book button marks a draft) sits between the greeting and the level card and pushes everything down by about 230 pt. Debug → `Journal time` (`Real / Evening / Daytime / Cleared`; its accessibility label is still `Journal card`) forces either state, so positions below hold for `Daytime` (verified at 08:08 on the real clock: no card, the level card directly under the greeting). The greeting (`Good morning, let's level up`) follows the real clock, not this setting.

- **Open the card.** Scroll until a quest row is visible (rows are tinted, pill shows a range such as `5–15`). Scroll with a swipe that starts around y=760 pt and ends at 200–300 pt. Tap the quest's round button (the first tap after a card closes or the app launches is often swallowed; screenshot and repeat). A centered card appears on a dimmed page with the quest's disc and title, a `?` box, `Complete` and `Not yet` (the tab bar fades out). A tap on the dim, like `Not yet`, closes it and leaves the ledger alone.
- **Complete.** Tap `Complete` (about x=220 pt, y=545 pt for a one-line title). The card turns in place: range pill and blurred numbers scrolling (about 1.5 s), then `+<points>` with `paid` (a tap on the dim skips the roll), then after 0.9 s `how did that feel?` with five round buttons and `Done`. Capture `V shot today-complete-reveal`.
- **Rate.** Tap a button (the right-most is `Loved it`: about 320, 519 pt on a quest card), then `Done` (about 220, 574 pt). The rating row is written when the card closes. The 8 s idle timer starts when the rate phase appears and restarts on every pick, and the control tool plus a screenshot read takes longer than that: **reading a screenshot between `Complete` and the thumb loses the rating** (the card closes and the taps land on the page). Take the screenshot for the proof first, or tap `Complete` → thumb → `Done` back to back using the coordinates above. Driven again on 2026-10-07: with a 4 s wait and `Complete` (220, 550), `Loved it` (320, 525), `Done` (220, 579) on a card whose title wrapped to two lines the rating was written (`2 | quest | <text> | 1`); the same sequence with a 3 s wait and the one-line coordinates (220, 545 / 320, 519 / 220, 574) wrote the quest (`+6`) but **no rating row**, so the card position moves a few points with the title and the wait needs to be at least the roll plus the 0.9 s landing. Read the card's `Complete` position from a screenshot before tapping it. For a routine card the confirm card's `Complete` is about 220, 493 and the rating buttons are about 320, 540 and 220, 595.
- **Read the stored result.** Run `V ledger | tee build/verify-evidence/today-complete-ledger.txt`. A `quest` row for today with the points shown on the card appears, and the balance equals 100 + those points. Run `V sql "select ZRATING, ZTARGETKINDRAW, ZTEXTSNAPSHOT, ZQUESTID is not null from ZQUESTRATING"`: one row, rating `2`, `quest`, the quest's text, `1`.
- **Check the page.** Screenshot Today: the level card's coins equal the ledger balance, the hand line ends `1 day streak`, `Completed` reads `1 done · +<points>` and its top card shows a drawn check, a struck-through title and `+<points>` in its pill. Capture `V shot today-complete-after`.
- **Menu purchase.** Tap `⋯` on an open quest row: `Reroll` (pill `10 coins`), `Cancel` (greyed while the balance is too low, pill kept, e.g. `120 coins`), `Replace`. Tap `Reroll`: a card `Reroll?` with "Swap it for a different <E/M/H> for 10 coins. The next reroll of this slot today costs more." (the tier appears as its slot letter), `Spend 10`, `Not yet`. Tap `Spend 10` (about 220, 503 pt). `V ledger` shows a `reroll` row of `-10` dated the app day, note `Reroll: <old title>`; `V sql "select ZSLOTRAW, ZTEXTSNAPSHOT, ZREPLACED, ZREPLACEDREASONRAW, ZREROLLCOUNT from ZDAILYQUEST where ZDAYKEY='<day>'"` keeps the old row with `ZREPLACED=1` and `rerolled`, and adds a new row of the same tier with `ZREROLLCOUNT=1`. At Lv 3 the first reroll is `free` and the button reads `Use free reroll`.
- **Routine.** Tap the round button on a routine row: the card has the routine's disc and title, a `+<n>` pill and no `?` box. `Complete` turns it (a short fade, no roll) into the big `+<n>`, `paid`, `how did that feel?` and `Done`; `<n>` equals the `routine` row in `V ledger`. A rating is filed as `routine` in `ZQUESTRATING` with `ZQUESTID` = the occurrence's id. The same holds for `Do now` on an Ahead row (needs a Saturday or `V day`).
- **Add for today (library).** Tap `+`: a sheet `Add for today` with `Cancel`, a pill picker `From library` / `Custom`, the caption "Extra work on top of today. It replaces nothing, doesn't block the hidden quest and costs nothing if left undone. Done, it pays like any routine.", a `Routine` list with a `Search library` field (rows show `+N`; routines already on the page sit under `Already on today's page`, each with a `Done · N` button that raises an alert `Mark as done?` with `Complete` / `Cancel` and, confirmed, completes the routine with no payout card and closes the sheet, or a `+points` pill once done), and a disabled `Add` pill at the bottom. Tap a row (about 220, 395 pt for the first), then `Add` (about 220, 888 pt). The sheet closes and the row appears under Routines. `V sql "select ZTEXTSNAPSHOT, ZBASEPOINTS, ZDUEDAYKEY, ZCOUNTSFORCLEAR, ZROUTINEID is null, ZADHOCSOURCEROUTINEID is not null from ZROUTINEOCCURRENCE order by Z_PK desc limit 1"` returns the routine's text and base points, the app day, `0`, `1`, `1`; the ledger is unchanged until it is completed.
- **Add for today (custom, from source, not driven).** `Custom`: a `What needs doing` field, difficulty `Easy` / `Medium` / `Hard` (pays 10 / 21 / 38 at a normal tier), a doodle grid, a `Similar in your library` card with `Use this`, and a `Save to library` toggle (`Random quest` / `Routine` with schedule options) that also writes a template or routine row. An extra's `⋯` has a free `Remove`.

## Gotchas

- Payout points are drawn once at completion, within the band on the row's pill (e.g. `5–15`). Assert the band and that the card, the row and the ledger agree — not a fixed number.
- Completion is final. To repeat the recipe use `V up --fresh`, not Debug → Reset today / Reopen quests (those are test aids, not an app behavior to prove; see [day-rollover](./day-rollover.md)).
- A rating is optional: the card closes by itself 8 s after the rate phase appears or after the last pick, and the rating row exists only if one was picked.
- The streak counts any completed non-epic, non-replaced random quest (hidden and micro-action ones included); routines and the epic do not produce `1 day streak`.
- The hidden gate (from source, not driven): `Clear every slot to unlock` opens only when every non-hidden random quest is done **and** every routine due today that counts toward clearing is done or skipped, with at least one random slot. Overdue routines not due today do not gate it. It then reads `Reveal the hidden quest`, with no confirm card.
- A seed quest called `Write a journal entry` shows a prompt line under its title (e.g. `The thing that drained me most today`) and is an ordinary quest: completing it does not open the editor.
- The level card also carries a `N day streak` pill once the streak is above 0, the hand line reads `Sun · Oct 11` (plus `· N day streak`), and the greeting switches to `Good afternoon` / `Good evening` with `wrap up` from 18:00. While the balance is negative the level card's coins turn clay (driven after `V day 4`: `-99 coins`).
- A level-up or streak-milestone card can pop up after the rating card closes and blocks the next tap (from the source; not reached in this pass).
- A scroll gesture opened the Levels sheet once right after launch (a swipe from 700 to 160 pt); it did not repeat. If a sheet appears, close it with `Done` and scroll again.
- Overdue (`overdue · day 2`) needs `V day <N>`; the Ahead section does not (it is there on any day Monday to Saturday, see `today-ahead`). The swipe actions are gone: nothing on Today responds to a swipe except scrolling.
- Every state of the cards without time travel: Settings → Design gallery (the row sits at about y = 795 pt at the top of Settings; check a screenshot). Sections, top to bottom: `Completed stack` (FIRST), `Ahead stack`, colours, type … and `Today cards` last. A flick from y=800 to y=120 scrolls about one screen. Start swipes above y=840: the floating bar swallows gestures that begin on it.
