# Today: complete a quest

The Today tab is a scroll of cards: header (avatar, a round book button that is not drawn while the evening journal card shows, `+`), greeting, a dark journal card (`tonight` from 20:00, `today` when shown by day because the day is cleared), the level card, an `Epic` section with a routine-style card (tap the body to expand), Routines (full-width rows, overdue ones pinned first), Today's quests (full-width tinted rows), a `Hidden` section, then Completed and Ahead this week (each a stack). There is no Backlog section: missed routines show on their due day's sheet on the Calendar. The round complete button on a quest or routine row asks for confirmation, writes a ledger entry, plays the payout reveal (quests only; a routine's fixed payout goes straight to `+N` and `paid`) and offers a rating, all in one centered card. The coin count on the level card must equal the ledger.

## Sub-features

- `today-render` shows the level card, the epic card, `Routines`, `Today's quests` for the app day.
- `today-complete` completes a random slot: the centered card (`?` + `Complete` + `Not yet`), then in place rolling, landed (`+N`), rate; ledger row, level card update, hand line `1 day streak`.
- `today-rate` logs a rating (−2…+2) from the card's rate phase. There is no `Done`: a pick closes the card about 0.5 s later and saves the rating (a second pick inside that half second replaces the first); with no pick the card closes after 8 s, and a tap on the dim closes it at once, keeping a pick already made and otherwise writing no rating.
- `today-routine-complete` completes a routine row (open, and its late/lighter variants).
- `today-menu` the `⋯` on a row or the epic card opens a popover (a long-press on the card opens the system context menu instead, with rows titled `Reroll · 10` / `Reroll · free` and no price pill; a blocked row is just disabled). Quest: `Reroll`, `Cancel`, `Replace`, `Rate / note`, `Turn off`, then `Open link` when the quest has one. Routine (in the order the source builds it): `Cancel`, `Replace`, `Remove` (extras only), `Rate / note`, `Turn off`. Epic: `Reroll`, `Extend`, `Replace`, `Rate / note`, `Turn off`, `Open link`. Only `Reroll`, `Cancel` and `Extend` carry a price pill (`10 coins`, `free`); a blocked one is greyed and keeps it. Items are conditional: `Cancel` only on Easy / Medium / Hard quests and fixed routines (200 coins for a routine), `Replace` only where the add rules allow, and `Reroll` / `Extend` disappear once done; `Turn off` becomes `Turn on` for a row that is off (pill `turned off`). A hidden quest's menu is `Rate / note` and `Turn off`, a micro-action group's is `Reroll` and `Replace`, an Ahead row's is `Replace`, `Rate / note` and `Turn off`. Rows in the Completed stack carry only `Rate / note`, except a done epic with a link, which keeps `Open link`.
- `today-ahead` the `Ahead this week` header (`6 open`, clay `−13 Sun night`) over a stack of rows; tap the stack or the header to fan out; while it is open the header itself reads `Collapse` (the `N open` and the clay figure show only while it is folded; since commit 67e8aa4 there is no separate `Collapse` button) and folds it; `Do now` on a candidate raises the same centered card (header, `+N` pill, `Complete`, `Not yet`) and a `routine` ledger row follows. On a low or very-low day a routine with a light version asks `Did the original` / `Did a lighter version` instead of `Complete`. Weekdays start collapsed (driven on a Wednesday: `5 open`, tap the header at about 90, 667 pt and the rows fan out, the chevron turns), Saturday and Sunday open. Candidates are the active flexible routines still short of their target with a later due day this week, so there are no candidates on a Sunday, but the section can still show there with earlier days' open rows and the clay figure; the clay `−N Sun night` figure counts only open rows from earlier days, and is gone inside a [recovery](./recovery.md) stretch.
- `today-add` the `+` sheet (`Add for today`) adds extra work on top of the day: `From library` (a routine) or `Custom`. Two pills sit at the bottom on both tabs, `Add` (leaves the extra open) and `Add & done` (records it as finished at once, closes the sheet and raises the same reward card a routine completion shows, with its fixed payout); both stay dim until a row is picked or a task is typed. There is no confirmation line above them, except `Saved to your library` when a Custom task is being saved to the library (the `Replace` sheet keeps its `… · can't be undone` bar). Driven: the library path (the sheet and its caption; the add itself in an earlier pass) and, by the owner on 2026-10-12, the Custom tab with `Add` and `Add & done`.
- `today-journal` the book button and the dark journal card open the journal editor (see [journal-editor](./journal-editor.md)). The button is not drawn while the card shows, so in the evening (or once the day is cleared) the card is the only way in from Today. The card's subtitle `A blank page.` shows only while nothing is written; its button reads `Start writing`, `Keep going · N words` or, after the editor was closed with writing in it, `Written · N words`.
- `today-levels` the level card (or the avatar, about 44, 92 pt) opens the Levels sheet (driven): `Levels`, a standing card (`Lv 1`, `0 earned`, `60 to Lv 2`, 20 dots), `Perks` with seven locked rows (`The day's first reroll is free` Lv 3, `The epic can be extended three times` Lv 5, `A late make-up pays 60% instead of 50%` Lv 8, `One free streak freeze a month` Lv 10, `Epic reroll 80 → 40` Lv 12, `The day's first two rerolls are free` Lv 15, `Streak freeze 300 → 150` Lv 20, each with `Lv N · X earned`) and a full-width `Done` pill at the bottom (about 220, 890 pt).
- `today-completed` a done card stays in its own section for about 0.9 s (check, strikethrough and `+N` finish there) and then moves to the `Completed` stack after Today's quests / Hidden: header `<n> done · +<sum>` equals today's done ledger rows, with two exceptions the source makes (the live epic is counted even when it was done on an earlier day this week, and a routine done ahead stays in Ahead), tap the stack or the header to fan out, the header (reading `Collapse` while open) to fold, collapsed again after `V relaunch`. The section it left reads `<n> / <m> done`, or `cleared` when nothing is open.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open the app; the Today tab is first (x=100, y=880 pt).
- Tap the round button on a quest or routine row.
- Tap `⋯` on a row or the epic card (or long-press it); tap `+` (Add for today) top-right (about 396, 92 pt).
- Tap the level card or the avatar (about 44, 92 pt) to open the level track sheet.
- Tap the book button (about 342, 92 pt, accessibility label `Journal`; absent while the dark card shows) or the dark journal card for the journal.

## Driving it with the simulator

Preconditions:

- `V up --fresh`; `V doctor` shows `offset: 0` and note `app day`.
- The level card reads `100 coins`, `60 to Lv 2`, `normal day`, and `<n> random slots` (`1 random slot` when it is one); the hand line has no streak part. The slot count follows the day's routine load: 3 on a quiet weekday, 1 on a Saturday (6 routines), 2 on a Sunday (4); Wednesday 2026-10-07 had 3 routines and 2 random slots, plus the epic.
- From 20:00 until 05:00 (or once the day is cleared) a dark journal card (script label `tonight`, `Write about today`, the subtitle `A blank page.` only while nothing is written, then `Start writing`, or `Keep going · N words` / `Written · N words` once there is text) sits between the greeting and the level card and pushes everything down by about 230 pt (measured before its subtitle was shortened and dropped once text exists; re-measure). The book button is not drawn while it shows; when it is, a purple dot on it marks a draft. Debug → `Journal time` (`Real / Evening / Daytime / Cleared`; its accessibility label is still `Journal card`) forces either state, so positions below hold for `Daytime` (verified at 08:08 on the real clock: no card, the level card directly under the greeting). The greeting (`Good morning, let's level up`) follows the real clock, not this setting.

- **Open the card.** Scroll until a quest row is visible (rows are tinted, pill shows a range such as `5–15`). Scroll with a swipe that starts around y=760 pt and ends at 200–300 pt. Tap the quest's round button (the first tap after a card closes or the app launches is often swallowed; screenshot and repeat). A centered card appears on a dimmed page with the quest's disc and title, a `?` box, `Complete` and `Not yet` (the tab bar fades out). A tap on the dim, like `Not yet`, closes it and leaves the ledger alone.
- **Complete.** Tap `Complete` (about x=220 pt, y=545 pt for a one-line title). The card turns in place: range pill and blurred numbers scrolling (about 1.5 s), then `+<points>` with `paid` (a tap on the dim skips the roll), then after 0.9 s `how did that feel?` with five round buttons (nothing else; the card closes on a pick). Capture `V shot today-complete-reveal`.
- **Rate.** Tap a thumb (the right-most is `Loved it`). The card closes about 0.5 s later and the rating row is written then; there is no `Done` to tap. The 8 s idle timer starts when the rate phase appears and restarts on every pick, and the control tool's round trips are slower than that: **reading a screenshot between `Complete` and the thumb loses the rating** (the card closes and the tap lands on the page; three ratings were lost that way on 2026-10-12). Take the proof screenshot (`V shot`, which is host-side and fast) first, or tap `Complete`, wait about 3 s (the 1.5 s roll plus the 0.9 s landing) and tap the thumb, back to back. The card is centred and its height follows the title, so the thumb row's y moves with the title length (on 2026-10-07 a 3 s wait with one-line coordinates on a two-line title wrote the quest but no rating). The coordinates measured while a `Done` pill sat under the thumbs (thumb about 320, 519 on a one-line quest card; `Done` 220, 574) no longer hold, since the card is shorter now: calibrate on the first completion (let it time out; a rating is optional) with `V shot`, and reuse the result for a card with the same title length. The proof screenshot after the pick shows Today, not the card; prove the rating with the query below. For a routine card the confirm card's `Complete` is about 220, 493, and the thumbs are already up when the card turns (no roll), so the 8 s counts from `Complete`.
- **Read the stored result.** Run `V ledger | tee build/verify-evidence/today-complete-ledger.txt`. A `quest` row for today with the points shown on the card appears, and the balance equals 100 + those points. Run `V sql "select ZRATING, ZTARGETKINDRAW, ZTEXTSNAPSHOT, ZQUESTID is not null from ZQUESTRATING"`: one row, rating `2`, `quest`, the quest's text, `1`.
- **Check the page.** Screenshot Today: the level card's coins equal the ledger balance, the hand line ends `1 day streak`, `Completed` reads `1 done · +<points>` and its top card shows a drawn check, a struck-through title and `+<points>` in its pill. Capture `V shot today-complete-after`.
- **Menu purchase.** Tap `⋯` on an open quest row: `Reroll` (pill `10 coins`), `Cancel` (greyed while the balance is too low, pill kept, e.g. `120 coins`), `Replace`. Tap `Reroll`: a card `Reroll?` with "Swap for a different <E/M/H>, 10 coins. The next one costs more." (the tier appears as its slot letter), `Spend 10`, `Not yet`. Tap `Spend 10` (about 220, 503 pt). `V ledger` shows a `reroll` row of `-10` dated the app day, note `Reroll: <old title>`; `V sql "select ZSLOTRAW, ZTEXTSNAPSHOT, ZREPLACED, ZREPLACEDREASONRAW, ZREROLLCOUNT from ZDAILYQUEST where ZDAYKEY='<day>'"` keeps the old row with `ZREPLACED=1` and `rerolled`, and adds a new row of the same tier with `ZREROLLCOUNT=1`. At Lv 3 the first reroll is `free` and the button reads `Use free reroll`.
- **Routine.** Tap the round button on a routine row: the card has the routine's disc and title, a `+<n>` pill and no `?` box. `Complete` turns it (a short fade, no roll) into the big `+<n>`, `paid`, `how did that feel?` and the five thumbs; `<n>` equals the `routine` row in `V ledger`. A rating is filed as `routine` in `ZQUESTRATING` with `ZQUESTID` = the occurrence's id. The same holds for `Do now` on an Ahead row (needs a Saturday or `V day`).
- **Add for today (library).** Tap `+`: a sheet `Add for today` with `Cancel`, a pill picker `From library` / `Custom`, the caption "Extra work. No penalty if you skip it.", a `Routine` list with a `Search library` field (rows show `+N`; routines already on the page sit under `Already on today's page` with the line "Did one of these? Mark it done here.", each with a `Done · N` button that raises an alert `Mark as done?` (message: the title, then "This can't be undone.") with `Complete` / `Cancel` and, confirmed, completes the routine with no payout card and closes the sheet, or a `+points` pill once done), and two dim pills at the bottom, `Add` and `Add & done` (side by side, stacked when the labels no longer fit; read their position from a screenshot). Tap a row (about 220, 395 pt for the first), then `Add`. The sheet closes and the row appears under Routines. `V sql "select ZTEXTSNAPSHOT, ZBASEPOINTS, ZDUEDAYKEY, ZCOUNTSFORCLEAR, ZROUTINEID is null, ZADHOCSOURCEROUTINEID is not null from ZROUTINEOCCURRENCE order by Z_PK desc limit 1"` returns the routine's text and base points, the app day, `0`, `1`, `1`; the ledger is unchanged until it is completed. With `Add & done` instead (from source for this tab; owner-driven on Custom) the sheet closes onto the routine's reward card (big `+N`, thumbs, same 8 s rule as above), the occurrence is completed in the same step and `V ledger` gains a `routine` row for N, the fixed pay (`ZAWARDEDPOINTS` on the occurrence).
- **Add for today (custom).** `Custom`: a `Task` row (the suggested doodle and a `What needs doing` field), `Difficulty` `Easy` / `Medium` / `Hard` with the line `Pays N.` (10 / 21 / 38 at a normal tier), a `Save to library` card (`Random quest` / `Routine` with schedule options; it also writes a template or routine row, and its summary reads `Pays by tier.` for a quest and `<when> · pays N.` for a routine), a collapsed `Change icon` row (the doodle in a disc and a chevron; tapping it opens the doodle grid and picking one closes it again), and, when the text resembles a library routine, a `Similar in your library` card with `Use this` and the line "Doesn't count toward a weekly target." Driven by the owner on 2026-10-12: `Add` and `Add & done` here; the save-to-library, `Change icon` grid and `Similar` paths are from source. An extra's `⋯` has a free `Remove`.

## Gotchas

- Payout points are drawn once at completion, within the band on the row's pill (e.g. `5–15`). Assert the band and that the card, the row and the ledger agree — not a fixed number.
- Completion is final. To repeat the recipe use `V up --fresh`, not Debug → Reset today / Reopen quests (those are test aids, not an app behavior to prove; see [day-rollover](./day-rollover.md)).
- A rating is optional: the card closes 0.5 s after a pick, or by itself 8 s after the rate phase appears when nothing is picked, and the rating row exists only if one was picked.
- The streak counts any completed non-epic, non-replaced random quest (hidden and micro-action ones included); routines and the epic do not produce `1 day streak`.
- The hidden gate (from source, not driven): `Clear every slot to unlock` opens only when every non-hidden random quest is done **and** every routine due today that counts toward clearing is done or skipped, with at least one random slot. Overdue routines not due today do not gate it. It then reads `Reveal the hidden quest`, with no confirm card.
- A seed quest called `Write a journal entry` shows a prompt line under its title (e.g. `The thing that drained me most today`) and is an ordinary quest: completing it does not open the editor.
- The streak shows only in the greeting's hand line, `Sun · Oct 11` plus `· N day streak` once it is above 0 (the level card's streak pill was removed in d977463), and the greeting switches to `Good afternoon` / `Good evening` with `wrap up` from 18:00. With a name ([profile](./profile.md)) it reads `Good evening, <name>,` (driven 2026-10-12: `Good evening, Ada,`). Inside a recovery stretch a pill `Recovery · through <Mon d>` sits under the greeting and the level card reads `very low day` ([recovery](./recovery.md)). A slot the re-plan dropped is not listed at all. While the balance is negative the level card's coins turn clay (driven after `V day 4`: `-99 coins`).
- Shortened Today copy (d977463, 2c3638f, dbaa00e): the empty random list reads "Nothing to draw today. The day is yours.", a failed hidden reveal "Nothing left to reveal.", a refused reroll shows its reason alone (no "Nothing was charged"), a turned-off quest says `Turned off`, an extra's row note ends `· extra`, and the Cancel confirmations read "Drop it for N coins. It earns nothing and won't count for the streak." (quest) and "Drop it for N coins. No more deductions." (routine). The re-plan alert (`Re-plan what's open` / `Leave it`; not driven) now has a short body ("Readings now say low, not normal." plus counts such as "2 quests stay.", "1 quest would be dropped."), and `Leave it` is remembered for that day and tier: the foreground re-read does not offer the same change again today (`V pref declinedReplans` holds the remembered change), while another tier or a later day asks again.
- A level-up or streak-milestone card can pop up after the rating card closes and blocks the next tap (from the source; not reached in this pass).
- A scroll gesture opened the Levels sheet once right after launch (a swipe from 700 to 160 pt); it did not repeat. If a sheet appears, close it with `Done` and scroll again.
- Overdue (`overdue · day 2`) needs `V day <N>`; the Ahead section does not (it is there on any day Monday to Saturday, see `today-ahead`). The swipe actions are gone: nothing on Today responds to a swipe except scrolling.
- Every state of the cards without time travel: Settings → Design gallery (scroll Settings to the bottom; the row sits at about y = 590 pt there; check a screenshot). Sections, top to bottom: `Completed stack` (FIRST), `Ahead stack`, colours, type … and `Today cards` last. A flick from y=800 to y=120 scrolls about one screen. Start swipes above y=840: the floating bar swallows gestures that begin on it.
